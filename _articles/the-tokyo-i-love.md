If I choose a time when few people are out and about, and I walk through back lanes where there are few passersby, it is often hard to believe that I am in the midst of metropolitan Tokyo. And I am astonished by the fact that a deep evergreen broadleaf forest could have been planted here during the Taishō era,30 from trees donated by people throughout Japan. It’s a fine example of how a forest can be created in a short time, provided people have the will to do so.
